RETURN & REFUND POLICY
======================
Last updated: January 2025

DIGITAL PRODUCTS
----------------
All digital products (software licenses, subscriptions, downloadable content)
are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of purchase if:
- The product did not work as described.
- You experienced a technical issue that our support team could not resolve.

To request a refund, email billing@company.com with your order number and reason.
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.

PHYSICAL PRODUCTS
-----------------
Physical items may be returned within 60 days of delivery in original condition.
Items must be unused and in original packaging. Return shipping is free for
defective items. Customers pay return shipping for change-of-mind returns.

NON-REFUNDABLE ITEMS
---------------------
- Custom or personalised orders.
- Downloadable content that has already been accessed more than 3 times.
- Subscriptions cancelled after the 30-day window (access continues to period end).

HOW TO INITIATE A RETURN
-------------------------
1. Email returns@company.com with your order number.
2. Our team will respond within 2 business days with a Return Merchandise
   Authorisation (RMA) number.
3. Ship the item back using the provided label.
4. Refund is issued once we receive and inspect the item.

EXCHANGES
---------
Exchanges for a different product are available within 30 days. Contact
support@company.com to arrange an exchange.
